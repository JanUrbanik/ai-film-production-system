# Environment Bible Engine

**Owner seat:** R4  
**Template:** `04_Bibles/templates/ENVIRONMENT_BIBLE.md`

## Stages

1. **Extract** locations, TOD, weather, landmarks from beats.  
2. **Path A Establishing** — wide masters multi-angle.  
3. **Path B Zones** — interior corners / exterior beats used on camera.  
4. **Path C Landmarks & materials** — recurring details.  
5. **Lighting matrix** — L_day / L_magic / L_night as needed.  
6. **Lock** — `loc-vN` version; continuity rules written.  
7. **Critic** — landmark/TOD consistency across plates.  
8. **Publish** LOCKED + env preamble.

## MVP

One location, 2–4 plates minimum.
