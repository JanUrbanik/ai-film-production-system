# TRANSCRIPT — porch_letter

The corridor is empty. Dust hangs in a beam of warm light from a wall sconce. At the far end an open dark-wood doorway shows a cool dusk sky and the silhouettes of trees.

Miranda Cole stands just inside the doorway, a wool charcoal coat still on, a sealed letter in her right hand. She looks back down the corridor, then at the letter.

She walks toward the doorway. The floorboards creak. A thin curtain at the door edge stirs once.

"You came back," she says, barely above a whisper. No one answers.

Miranda turns the letter over. The paper is cheap, cream, edges soft from being carried. She does not open it.

Night presses in through the doorway. She steps onto the porch, the letter still unopened, and the corridor behind her goes still.
